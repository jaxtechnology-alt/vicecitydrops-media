Privacy policy

The "Vice City Drops Comments" app is used only by the owner of the Vice City Drops YouTube channel to read comments on the channel's own videos and post replies from that channel, using the YouTube Data API.

It does not collect, sell or share personal data. Comment text that is publicly visible on our videos is read only to write replies. No data is used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
